## Findings (all four standing questions answered)

1. **Penny Royal shard re-integration** — a swarm mind's fragments are
   divergent *states of consciousness*, not copies. Its evil was
   compartmentalized in a state Amistad labelled "Eight"; a curator can
   sever a pathological state and revive the rest. Re-merge is NOT
   trustless — curation/excision precedes reassembly, and severed shards
   persist as fugitive assets (Penny Royal hunted its own eighth across
   Masada). It also leaves autonomous custodian shards (the dark diamond
   with Blite).
2. **Orlandine subpersona fate** — scaffolding, not permanent: she
   "absorbed her subpersona and realized she would never need to rely on
   such constructs again"; by *The Human* she is "more Jain tech than
   human," commanding platform AIs directly. (Warship/Human-specific text
   partially unverified — flagged in the log per intake discipline.)
3. **Owner-trilogy Committee** — the pre-coup government was already a
   mechanized delegation machine (Inspectorate, shepherds, cams,
   political officers) that had delegitimized itself; the AIs inherited
   contempt and machine-conditioned subjects. Earth Central began as "an
   electronic bureaucrat."
4. **Brockle forensic method** — swarm-body interrogator: forced
   perfect-recall replay under twisted perspective, cross-subject
   comparison, VR scenarios, memplant ingestion. Kept confined for
   same-kind insight — and it had prepared countermeasures against its
   own leash.

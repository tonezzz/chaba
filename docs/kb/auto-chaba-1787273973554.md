### Implementation
Made the 12 GiB zram swap persistent on tony-omen. Created /usr/local/bin/zram-swap-setup.sh, /usr/local/bin/zram-swap-teardown.sh, and a systemd unit /etc/systemd/system/zram-swap.service that initializes /dev/zram0 (12 GiB, priority 100) at boot. Stopped the SDXL Uvicorn process on port 8000 (pt_main_thread, pid 3974) which was consuming 8.3 GiB of swap. Swap usage dropped from 46 GiB to 35 GiB and free swap increased from ~1 GiB to 24 GiB after adding zram and killing SDXL.

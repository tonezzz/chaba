# Dispatch outcome — cms-auto-nest stale investigation (2026-10-13)

## Verdict

**Not a daemon outage — an MDDB migration split-brain.** All three
`nest-mgr.service` daemons were healthy and publishing every 15 min all
day — into the *parked* `:11023` DB after MEM-0545 moved the CMS plane to
`mddb-cms :11027` (~08:07 ICT, commit `9a1f6b7e1`). The installed copies
(`~/.local/share/nest/nest-mgr.py`) still ran the Oct-9 build with
`MDDB_BASE_URL=:11023`. The staleness threshold is **sane** — the
registry on :11027 genuinely was stale; `cms-auto-health` fired at the
first check past the 15m+30m grace (09:00) and auto-closes on recovery.

## Evidence

- **Timer state**: `nest-mgr.service` enabled + `active (running)`
  continuously on dell (3d3h), omen (1d23h), idc03. Journal quiet because
  `print("published")` is block-buffered; stderr tick-errors appear only
  on real failures.
- **Last publish**: registry `last_run` on :11027 froze at
  `2026-10-13T01:0x` UTC. The same pages on **:11023** show refresh
  entries every ~15 min through `12:23` UTC — the daemons were publishing
  into the dead letterbox.
- **:11027 reachability**: up since 08:07:41 ICT; served writes all day
  (digests 15:01, audit batch 16:35, flood/camwall jobs 19:00+;
  `cms-auto-health` on dell read it every 15 min). The `Connection
  refused` tick errors at **15:34–15:40 on all three hosts** (including
  idc03 itself) targeted **:11023**, the old code's endpoint — a brief
  primary-mdb flap, only visible because a failed `get_registry` forces
  a publish attempt every tick.
- **Recovery**: repointed script redeployed via `install.sh` at 19:31
  ICT, services restarted 19:33 → all three published to :11027 at
  12:33 UTC; `last_run` advancing normally (12:48–12:49 UTC at check).
- **Name correction**: the stale pages are generated by
  `stacks/services/nest-mgr/nest-mgr.py`, not `scripts/ada/nest-overview.py`
  (which owns `chaba-nest`, repo-run, unaffected).

## Residual finding (carded)

Parked `:11023` collections still **accept writes** — other producers
with unit-level `:11023` pins are still publishing CMS pages there:
`jev-bench.service` (idc03, `ExecStart --mddb :11023` → `bench-jev`,
last write 12:47 UTC), `ops-health-report.service` (idc03 env),
`flood-alert.service` + `ada-flood-news.service` (dell env),
`ada-pi-orch-bench.service` (omen `--mddb`). Filed kanban card
**`cms-11023-parked-writers` (MEM-0594)** — sweep unit-level MDDB pins,
then delete parked collections after the rollback window.

Accepted triggers: `/archive` or the phrase `Let's archive.`
Note: `/summarize` now auto-archives unsaved KB-worthy facts after producing its summary. Use `/archive` standalone only when you want to archive without a status summary.

1. Review the current conversation for KB-worthy facts: decisions, discoveries, infrastructure changes, conventions, and workarounds.
2. Check existing memories to avoid duplicates; update or archive stale entries instead of creating duplicates.
3. Create or update memories in the correct corpus with clear titles and relevant tags.
4. Skip temporary commands, one-off output, and obvious trivia.
5. Report back with a summary of what was saved, updated, or archived.
6. End the response with the exact message: "Ready to be archived."

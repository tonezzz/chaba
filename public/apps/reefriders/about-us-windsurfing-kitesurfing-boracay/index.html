About Us

Welcome to Reef Riders Windsurfing, Kitesurfing & SUP Center in Boracay, Philippines! (fully operational from November to April)

Reef Riders is an authorized Starboard Windsurfing, Severne & Airush Kiteboarding & Wingfoiling Center in the Philippines. It began its operation on December 2009 and continuously strives to cater to the windsurfing, kitesurfing, wingfoiling and stand-up paddleboarding needs of the watersports lovers!

WINDSURFING, KITESURFING, WINGFOILING & STAND-UP PADDLEBOARDING LESSONS AND RENTALS

The center aims to provide beginner, intermediate and advanced rentals, beginner, intermediate and advanced lessons and a hospitable and accommodating crew for a convenient, fun, and unlimited action on the water using the best quality and latest Starboard, Severne & Airush equipment. The crew will gladly help you in choosing the right gear to suit your windsurfing & kitesurfing needs.

BOLABOG BEACH, BORACAY

Boracay has 2 seasons: Amihan Season (Northeast Monsoon) and Habagat Season (Southwest Monsoon). The main indicator of the switch between the 2 seasonal patterns is the change in the direction of the wind.

Amihan Season is characterized by moderate temperatures, little or no rainfall, and a prevailing wind from the east. This is the windsurfing season in Boracay; windsurfing in Bolabog Beach. It begins sometime in September or October and ends sometime in May or June. This however varies every year. So far, as observed, Boracay’s most windy months are during December, January and February. It’s during these months that a daily average of 20-30 knots of wind speed is common. 

Reef Riders’ main office is located on Bolabog Beach (Windsurfing, Kiteboarding & Wingfoiling Beach). Bolabog lagoon is protected by a reef from big waves making it an ideal place for the slalom and freestyle enthusiasts and beginners. Furthermore, the thrill-seeking windsurfers and kitesurfer have the option of going to the reef to get their dose of adrenaline rush from the bump and jump swells, especially when it is blowing 20-35 knots.

WHITE BEACH, BORACAY

For people who could only go to Boracay to windsurf & kiteboard during the Habagat Season (Southwest Monsoon, onshore wind on White Beach), we provide rentals and lesson on Sinagpa, White Beach (beachfront of Boracay Terraces Resort). Hence, we are open all year round!

WINGFOILING COURSES

Reef Riders Wingfoil Center and School offers one-on-one wingfoiling courses from beginner to advanced level, guaranteeing a well-supervised and client-centered training.

Shoes and harnesses are provided.

Courses are available daily for all levels.

Discovery Lesson: Learn to harness the power of the wind with a wing. Discovery Course starts on the beach learning to control the wing, and progresses to steering the wing while learning to balance and position on a big flat board on the water. 1 Hour.

Beginner Level 1: Learn to harness the power of the wind with a wing. The course starts on the beach learning to control the wing, and progresses to steering the wing while learning to balance and position on a big flat board on the water. 3 Hours.

Beginner Level 2: After you have mastered wingsurfing, then it’s time to get foiling. Harness the power of the wind and take off on foil to experience your first rides. 4 Hours.

Full Beginner Course:  Combination of learning to control the wing and the foil. 7 Hours.

Stand-up Paddleboarding

Stand-up Paddleboarding in Boracay, Philippines!

 

“It will take you approximately 1 hour on a calm flat water day with moderate paddling, from Reef Riders on Bolabog Beach to Puka Beach.”

“Burn fat, trim your muscles, train your balance and get the best core workout with the SUP. Enjoy the nature on a sunny and calm flat water day or do an intense workout session. Catch and ride some waves. Paddle to places you normally would never reach. Or just relax and enjoy the beautiful sunset.”

“It will take you 2-3 hours from Reef Riders on Bolabog Beach to Jony’s or Epic depending on weather and currents. The intense paddling part would be between Bolabog and Puka in the Amihan Season (October-April). Rent a board at www.reefriders-watersports.com and paddle from Bolabog to White Beach for your sunset drinks and dinner at www.jonysresort.com.”

-Ronald Schroeder

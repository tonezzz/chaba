1. Price levels

Gold and the two USD-cross rates on their natural scales. A stronger USD (higher USD/THB, higher USD/EUR) generally corresponds to lower gold prices.

2. Daily log returns

First differences of log prices. These are the stationary series used for the causality tests.

3. Strategy equity

Cumulative equity of the walk-forward THB→XAU strategy (2-day trend, 1-day horizon) versus buy-and-hold gold. Generated by trended.py.

3. Cross-correlation

XAU/USD vs USD/THB returns at lags -10 to +10. The strongest link is contemporaneous (lag 0, r ≈ -0.22).

4. Impulse response

Response of XAU/USD return to a one-unit shock in THB and USD/EUR returns from the VAR(4).

5. Key findings

  • Log levels for gold and THB are non-stationary; all log returns are stationary.
  • USD/THB returns strongly Granger-cause XAU/USD returns (p = 0.0).
  • USD/EUR returns also Granger-cause XAU/USD returns (p = 0.0149).
  • No evidence that XAU/USD returns lead USD/THB returns (p = 0.8862).
  • IRF: a THB shock lowers XAU by ~0.56 the next day, then the effect decays.

Note: These are exploratory, in-sample research results. Correlation/Granger causality does not prove economic causation or predictability out-of-sample.

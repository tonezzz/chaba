THB Settlement Choice

Compare the two ways to realize gold P/L: hold USD after the trade or convert immediately to THB. The curves are generated by the THB-base settlement variants. Research only.

SSOT: docs/ssot/jobs/experiments/2026-08-26-gold-thb-usd-settlement.yml

1. Settlement idea

The existing signal trades XAU/USD. After the trade, the app can choose to hold the USD proceeds or convert them to THB at the current USD/THB rate. The THB-base return is log(XAU/THB) = log(XAU/USD) + log(USD/THB). A THB-base position is therefore the gold return plus the USD/THB carry.

  • XAU/USD silver (ref): the current best XAU/USD silver graph+k-NN curve.
  • Hold USD, mark THB: XAU/USD position, but USD cash is revalued daily using USD/THB. The P/L is pos * xau_usd_next + usd_thb_next.
  • Settle to THB: XAU/THB position; the currency leg is marked daily. The P/L is pos * xau_thb_next.

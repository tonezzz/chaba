## lessons:

- opencode keeps full tool-call payloads in `~/.local/share/opencode/opencode.db`
  (`part` table, `state.input.content` for write) — artifact_not_durable
  misses are recoverable verbatim long after the worktree is gone.

  `sqlite3.connect("file:...?mode=ro", uri=True)`.
- Merge-held dispatch artifacts never reach origin — check session DB
  on the runner host before regenerating.
